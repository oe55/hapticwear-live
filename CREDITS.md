# Credits

## Music

Every built-in track is original, public domain, or licensed under Creative Commons Attribution
(CC BY). Each licence was checked on the track's own page before it was included.

**Changes made to every third-party track:** re-encoded to AAC (128 kbps) and its overall level
adjusted to match the others. Nothing was cut, edited or remixed.

Music in *My Library* is added by whoever runs the show computer. It stays in that browser and is
not part of this site.
